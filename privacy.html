01Who we are

Chug Jug PH (“we”, “us”) sells pre-stocked cocktail jugs with delivery in Metro Manila through this website. We are the personal information controller for the information described here, and we handle it in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

For anything about your personal data, contact us at hello@chugjugph.com. This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

04Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed for the purposes above:

  • Our team. Staff and authorized personnel who handle orders, payments and deliveries can see the information they need. Access to our back-office system requires a login, and each user is limited to the sections they have been permitted to use.
  • Riders and couriers. The person delivering your order receives your name, contact number, delivery address, notes and order contents.
  • Payment services. GCash and Maya process the payments you send, under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive the transfer details you or they provide to us (such as amount, reference and the screenshot you upload).
  • Technology providers that work for us.
    • Supabase, which hosts our database and file storage (orders, leads, notify requests, payment screenshots);
    • Vercel, which hosts the website;
    • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver fonts and a code library that the page loads. When your browser fetches these it contacts those services directly, so they receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Authorities and professional advisers. We may disclose information where required by law, a court order or a lawful request, or to protect our rights, customers or the public, and to our accountants or lawyers when needed.

Where data is processed. Our technology providers operate cloud infrastructure that may store or process data outside the Philippines. We choose established providers and use only the personal information needed for the service. The rules of the Data Privacy Act continue to apply to the information we collect.

If our business is ever sold or reorganized, customer information may be transferred as part of that process, and the protections in this policy will continue to apply.

05Browser storage and cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It does use your browser’s local storage, which stays on your device, for two things:

  • chugjug_cart remembers what is in your cart so it is still there if you refresh the page.
  • chugjug_cart_session is a random cart ID (not linked to your name) used so that, if we save an unfinished checkout, repeated saves from the same browser update one record instead of creating many.

You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings; doing so empties your cart. Your browser or device may also keep its own technical data such as cached files.

06How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes in this policy, and then delete it or anonymize it.

  • Orders, payment records and screenshots are kept for as long as we need to deliver and support the order, reconcile payments, handle any disputes, and meet tax, accounting and other legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Unfinished-checkout records and “Notify me” requests are kept only until we have followed up or the stock alert has been sent, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Messages to us are kept for as long as needed to resolve your query and for reasonable record-keeping.

07How we protect it

  • The website is served over HTTPS, so information you send is encrypted in transit.
  • Our back-office system requires a login and uses per-section permissions so people only see what they need.
  • Payment screenshots are stored in a private file store, not shown publicly on the website.
  • Order tracking requires both your order number and the contact number on the order.

No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

08Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is collected and used (this policy);
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Object to processing, including withdrawing consent you previously gave;
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information;
  • Erase or block your data in the circumstances the law allows, for example if it is incomplete, outdated, unlawfully obtained, or no longer needed;
  • Data portability, to obtain a copy of your data in a commonly used format where applicable;
  • Claim damages if you suffer harm from the inaccurate, unlawful or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission (see below).

How to use your rights

Email hello@chugjugph.com with what you would like us to do, and include your name, the contact number or email used at checkout, and an order number if you have one, so we can find and verify your records. We may ask for extra confirmation of identity before sharing or changing information. We will reply within a reasonable time. Some information (such as sales and payment records) we may be legally required to keep, and we will tell you if that applies.

To stop stock alerts, or unfinished-checkout follow-ups, simply tell us and we will delete the record.

09Age and children

Our products contain alcohol and our website is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the current version. If we make a significant change to how we use your personal information, we will highlight it on the website.

11Contact and complaints

Questions or requests about your personal data: hello@chugjugph.com, or message @chugjugph on social media.

If you believe your data privacy rights have been violated, you can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines at privacy.gov.ph. We would appreciate the chance to fix the problem first.
